Our Story

Founded in 2023 in Durban, Pawsitive Pet Academy is dedicated to improving animal welfare through accessible, high-quality training. Hundreds of pet owners and aspiring pet care professionals have completed both our six-month Professional Development Programmes and six-week Practical Skills Courses.

Our Mission

To empower pet parents and animal care professionals across Durban with practical skills, positive reinforcement methods, and deep industry knowledge.

Why Train With Us

  • Hands-on practical training environments
  • Industry-relevant course content
  • Experienced Durban-based instructors
